      Shields

      Every Runner starts with one shield - a dog's life. Miss a zone's window or get caught and it takes the hit; the second time either happens, you are out and join the Hunters. A shield spent on a catch also makes you immune to being caught again for a few minutes.

      Target Area

      Map is only visible to Hunters. As a Runner, your goal is to navigate to increasingly smaller zones that will be revealed during the game.
      The final zone is hidden somewhere in this circle, the same one for every Runner. Each of them closes in on it through their own zones, and you are never told where it is.
